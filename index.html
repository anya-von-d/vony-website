How It Works

How Vony Works for Friends and Family

01

Account Creation

Sign up and create secure profiles. Both lenders and borrowers verify their identity and link bank accounts for seamless, secure transactions.

02

Create Loan Offer

Lenders specify loan amount, payment plan and schedule, interest rate, and repayment period. The borrower receives the offer instantly in the app.

03

Review & Accept

Borrowers review offer details and complete a simple in-app contract: "I agree to repay this loan according to the agreed terms."

04

Loan Activated

Once accepted, the loan agreement is recorded in the system and funds are transferred securely through linked bank accounts.

05

Track Repayments

Borrowers send payments directly in the app according to the agreed schedule. Both parties track repayment progress in real time.
