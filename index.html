How It Works

Simple Steps to Start Lending or Borrowing

01

Account Creation

Sign up and create your secure profile. Verify your identity and link your bank account for seamless transactions.

02

Loan Offer

Lenders specify loan amount, payment schedule, interest rate, and repayment period. Borrowers receive offers instantly.

03

Loan Acceptance

Borrowers review offer details and complete a simple in-app contract agreement to proceed with the loan terms.

04

Active Loan & Repayment

Funds transfer securely between accounts. Both parties track repayment progress in real-time through the app.
