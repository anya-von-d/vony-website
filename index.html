How It Works

How Vony Works for Friends and Family

01

Create a Loan Offer

Enter the amount, repayment schedule, and whether interest is included. Vony will generate a loan offer and send it to the borrower.

02

Accept and Formalise

Once the borrower accepts, Vony creates a digital contract that both sides can access anytime.

03

Track and Repay

Repayments, balances, and history are clearly tracked so everyone knows what's been paid and what's still due.

No banks. No institutions. Just people lending to people, done properly.

Key Features

Flexible Lending Options

Custom Payment Plans

Set up flexible payment schedules that work for both parties. Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly payments with customisable amounts.

Interest Rate Options

Choose to lend with or without interest. Set fair rates for both parties or keep it simple with zero-interest loans between friends.

Repayment Periods

Set realistic timeframes from days to years. Perfect for short-term emergency loans or longer-term financial assistance.

Digital Contracts

Clear, legally-binding agreements created automatically. Both parties sign digitally with all terms clearly documented and stored securely.
